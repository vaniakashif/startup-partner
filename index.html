How FounderMatch works

From idea to partnership in five simple steps

1

Founder posts a startup idea

Describe your problem, solution, current stage, and what kind of partner you're looking for. Be specific about equity range and expected contribution.

2

Community members browse ideas

Filter by category, city, role needed, or stage. Find an idea that excites you and matches your skillset or resources.

3

Submit a proposal

Tell the founder what you bring — skills, capital, or network. Write a short pitch explaining why you're the right fit.

4

Founder reviews and selects

The founder reviews all proposals and reaches out to the most suitable candidates to begin conversations.

5

Discuss, agree, and build

Use our built-in chat and partnership document templates to finalise equity splits, roles, and begin building together.

RideShare for Students
University carpooling app connecting students commuting between campuses daily
TechMobilityB2C

The Problem

Students in Karachi spend 2–3 hours daily commuting. Public transport is unreliable and ride-hailing is expensive. There's no affordable, safe option designed specifically for students.

The Solution

A carpooling app exclusively for verified university students. Drivers and riders are both students, building trust through shared university identity. Payments are split automatically in-app.

Roles we're looking for

Tech Co-founder

Build and lead the mobile app (React Native or Flutter preferred)

5–10% equity
