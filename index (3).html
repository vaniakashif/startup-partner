About StartupPartner

We believe the best startup ideas don't always come from well-connected people. StartupPartner democratises startup building by connecting founders, talented professionals, and investors across Pakistan.

Why we started

Every week in Karachi, dozens of people pitch startup ideas. Most never build them — not because the ideas are bad, but because they can't find the right people to build with. We wanted to fix that.

Our mission

StartupPartner is the place where founders meet partners. Where developers find ideas that excite them. Where investors discover early-stage deals. Where anyone in Pakistan can build their next big thing.

How it works

1

Founder posts an idea

Describe your problem, solution, stage, and what kind of partner you need.

2

People browse ideas

Filter by category, city, role, or stage. Find an idea that excites you.

3

Submit proposals

Tell the founder what you bring — skills, capital, or network.

4

Founder reviews

Founder reviews proposals and reaches out to suitable candidates.

5

Build together

Finalize equity, roles, and start building your startup.
